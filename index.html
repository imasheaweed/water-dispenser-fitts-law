Fitts' Law: Smart Water Dispenser

Nowadays, people often multitask while getting water, such as holding a phone or other belongings while filling a bottle or paper cup. This makes us wonder: why are the water dispenser buttons sometimes placed so far from where we position our cup or bottle? Although many dispensers already use sensors to detect the cup or bottle, users still need to select the water temperature when multiple options are available. Since the cup or bottle may need to be held with one hand, having a larger button placed closer to the user could make the dispenser easier to operate with only one hand. In this case, Fitts’ Law is used to investigate how button size and vertical distance affect the performance of selecting the “Pour Water” button.

Experiment Setup

The experiment automatically tests 3 button sizes and 4 vertical distances, producing 12 conditions. Each condition contains 10 recorded trials.
